Slope & Rate of Change — Class Notes

Grade 7 · Unit: Linear Relationships · Name: ______________________ · Date: ____________

1. Key words

WordMeaning
RiseThe vertical change (up or down) between two points.
RunThe horizontal change (left or right) between two points.
SlopeHow steep a line is. Also called the rate of change.
Dependent / independenty is the dependent variable (it depends on x). x is the independent variable.

2. The slope formula

slope = rise ÷ run = vertical change ÷ horizontal change = Δy ÷ Δx = (y₂ − y₁) ÷ (x₂ − x₁)

Slope = change in the dependent variable ÷ change in the independent variable.

3. Four types of slope (read the line from left to right)

TypeWhat the line doesWhyExample
Positive ↗Goes upRise and run have the same sign3/2
Negative ↘Goes downRise and run have opposite signs−3/2
Zero →Flat (horizontal), like y = 4Rise = 0, and 0 ÷ run = 00
Undefined ↑Straight up and down (vertical), like x = 3Run = 0, and you can’t divide by 0undefined

Memory trick: Undefined = Up-and-down. Zero = flat like the floor.

4. Three ways to find slope

FromHowExample
A graph1) Pick two points on grid corners. 2) Count the rise (up +, down −). 3) Count the run (always to the right). 4) Divide rise ÷ run and simplify. Read the axis numbers: a square may be worth more than 1.Rise 4, run 6 → 4/6 = 2/3
Two pointsslope = (y₂ − y₁) ÷ (x₂ − x₁). Subtract in the same order on top and bottom.(−3, 1) and (2, 4): (4 − 1) ÷ (2 − (−3)) = 3/5
A tablePick two columns: change in y ÷ change in x. Check another pair: a constant rate gives the same slope.x: 1, 3, 5, 7 and y: 2, 8, 14, 20 → (8 − 2) ÷ (3 − 1) = 3

5. Rate of change in real life and steepness

Rate of change = change in the amount ÷ change in time, with units. A lift rises 450 m in 5 min → 450 ÷ 5 = 90 meters per minute. Decreasing amounts (draining, melting, sinking) have a negative rate.

Steepness: the size of the slope tells how steep (3 is steeper than 1/2, and −4 is steeper than 2). The sign tells the direction. Compare fractions with a common bottom: 3/4 vs 5/8 → 6/8 vs 5/8, so 3/4 is steeper.

6. Watch out!

✗ Run over rise. Slope is rise over run.✗ Lost sign. Downhill = negative slope.
✗ Mixed order of subtraction on top and bottom.✗ Not simplified. 6/9 should be 2/3.
